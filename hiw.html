StoryPulse

How It Works

Discover how to participate in our interactive storytelling platform
1

Read the Story

We release a new part of an ongoing story every week.
Read through the latest part of our featured story and discuss possible future scenarios. Each story segment comes with a cliffhanger, setting the stage for your predictions.

2

Make Your Prediction

Submit what you think will happen next in the story.
Use the comment box to submit your prediction before the countdown timer ends. Be creative! Accurate and detailed answers will also help like submissions preferred.

3

Wait for the Reveal

After the countdown ends, we release the next part.
Once the prediction period closes, we’ll publish the next segment of the story. An alert/email will let you know results are out and help you celebrate your submission!

4

Win Rewards

Match the author's continuation to earn special rewards.
If your prediction closely matches what happens in the story, receive exclusive rewards! Highlights and accuracy points go to the leaderboard. The higher your match, the greater the recognition as active readers might climb on our scoreboard!
